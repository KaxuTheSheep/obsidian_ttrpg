# Session 2: The Mantis and the Meridian

## Session Overview
- **Date:** 02/08/26
- **Location:** [[The Sunken Hollow|Sunken Hollow]] (Lower Level) → [[Solasfall]]
- **Session Duration:** ~7 hours
- **Party:** [[Bob]], [[Clara]], [[Carmilla]], [[Hjordis]], [[Leon]], [[Linguini]]

> **New Party Member:** [[Hjordis]] — a drenek who cannot swim, encountered mid-session in the Sunken Chamber.

---

## Lower Level Descent (Day 5, Morning)

### Location: Narrow Vent → Lower Passage

Starting from the Narrow Vent where Session 1 ended, the party descended into the Lower Passage.

**Environment:**
- Damp, low-ceilinged, dimmer than the upper levels
- Sparse bioluminescent fungus of a different strain
- Tight, claustrophobic atmosphere

**Encounter:** 2 Adult [[Cockroach|Cockroaches]]
- Fought in the confined Lower Passage
- No mana shells formed
- **Outcome:** Cockroaches killed without major difficulty

**Navigation:** Continued deeper into the Lower Passage → **Sunken Chamber**

---

## Location: Sunken Chamber (Day 5, Midday)

### Environment
- Small chamber with standing water pooling at its lowest point
- A land area with scattered debris
- Quiet, unnervingly still

### Discovery: Mass Death Site
On the dry land portion of the chamber, the party found a cluster of dead bodies:

- **Victims:** A mix of [[Order of the Ashen Oath]] members and **Ashen Followers**
- Bodies showed signs of violent death — not from each other
- All appeared to have been killed by something *else*

### The Mantises
Above the bodies, clinging to the ceiling / upper walls, were **two large praying mantises**:

- **Male Mantis** — smaller, with **chromatic skin** that shifted colour and texture
- **Female Mantis** — larger, more imposing also with **chromatic skin**

**Observed Behaviour:**
- The male descended and approached the female, displaying its chromatic skin in an elaborate courtship ritual
- The female showed no interest
- The female **broke the male's neck** and began eating it

**Combat Trigger:**
- The female mantis noticed the party during its meal
- Combat began against the **female mantis** 

**Combat Details:**
- Party fought the **female mantis** in the chamber
- During the fight, the party discovered **Hjordis** in the water
  - Hjordis was struggling in the standing water
  - Due to his **drenek lineage**, he cannot swim
  - Party rescued him during the combat
- **Outcome:** Female mantis defeated

---
