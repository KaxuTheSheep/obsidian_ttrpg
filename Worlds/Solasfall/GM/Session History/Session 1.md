# Session 1: Descent into the Sunken Hollow

## Session Overview
- **Date:** 13/07/26
- **Location:** [[Solasfall]] → [[The Sunken Hollow|Sunken Hollow]] ([[Hollowdeep]])
- **Session Duration:** 7 hours
- **Party:** [[Bob]], [[Clara]], [[Carmilla]], [[Leon]], [[Linguini]]

---

## Pre-Expedition

### The Night Before

The party spent the previous night at an inn in [[Solasfall]], approximately a 50-minute walk from the [[Worlds/Solasfall/Player/Lore/Rift|rift]] opening. They held a drinking feast, knowing it might be their last night on the surface.

**Party Status at Session Start:**
- **[[Bob]]:** Woke first, clear-headed
- **[[Clara]]:** Hungover/tired
- **[[Carmilla]]:** Hungover/tired
- **[[Leon]]:** Hungover/tired
- **[[Linguini]]:** Notably unaffected (didn't participate as heavily)

**Party Dynamic Notes:**

- All party members knew each other prior to the feast except [[Linguini]]
- [[Linguini]] was met during the feast and invited/kept along with the party

---

### The Walk to the [[Worlds/Solasfall/Player/Lore/Rift|Rift]] (Day 1, Morning)

[[Bob]] woke everyone up and they departed the inn for the rift opening.

**Journey Details:**
- 50-minute walk through Solasfall 
- Party moved with purpose, focused on reaching the rift
- Visual and sensory details of the city were noted but not investigated 
- Multiple hooks were presented but the party chose not to pursue them, prioritizing early rift entry

**Rift Arrival:**
- Large crowd of explorers gathered at the rift site
- Party used [[Skills|intimidation]] and other methods to push through the crowd
- Successfully reached approximately the first 5% of explorers near the rift
- Among the first groups to enter the rift

## The Sunken Hollow: Entry

### Entry Point: Rift Chamber (Day 1, Morning)

Upon entering the Hollowdeep, the party witnessed groups sprinting away from the entrance, heading in various directions.

**Party Decision:** Took the leftmost path → **Tunnel C (The Squeeze)**

- Smooth walls from water erosion

**Encounter:** Juvenile [[Cockroach]]
- Single juvenile [[cockroach]] encountered
- Had not yet formed its mana shell
- Party attempted to tame it but it resisted continuously
- **Outcome:** [[Cockroach]] killed

**Navigation:** Party continued left, entering Deep Tunnel D

## Location 2: Deep Tunnel D
**Discovery:** [[Mana stones]] found and collected
**Navigation:** Continued left, entering Transitional Passage

---

## Location 3: Transitional Passage
**Party Action:** Persisted in going left
**Missed Opportunity:** Hidden Portal (unnoticed, kept moving)
**Navigation:** Continued to Main Hall

## Location 4: Main Hall
**Encounter:** 3 Ant Soldiers
**Combat Details:**
- Party had numerical advantage
- Slight ambush advantage
- Ant Soldiers killed relatively easily

**Time Update:** End of combat marked the start of **Day 2**

---

## Location 5: [[Tarantula]] Burrow
### Entrance & Descent (Day 2, Morning)
**Path:** Party went left from Main Hall into the burrow
**Entry:**
- Carmilla slid down first, webs sticking to her, slowing her descent
- Carmilla became stuck in the webs
- Party used fire to burn the interlinked web structure to free her
- Rope was lowered from Linguini; Carmilla caught it upon freeing herself
- Fire carefully managed to avoid burning Carmilla

**Full Descent:**
- Rope secured in a stable location
- All party members descended into the main chamber

### Tarantula Burrow Chamber
**Chamber Details:**
- Large, dome-like room
- Two tunnels visible
- One large human-sized entangled cocoon present

**Cocoon:**
- Burned using Clara's [[Cindral#Emberwhip|Emberwhip]]
- Baby tarantulas burst out, most died, some lived and dealt with.

**Tunnel Choice:** Party went left

### The Ruined Tower (Day 2, Midday)
**Discovery:**
- Abandoned, ruined tower found in the left tunnel
- Structure appeared to be old/ruined

**Loot Found:**
- Jewelry
- Several weapons
- Lamp oil flask

**Decision:** Party rested in the tower
**Time Update:** Rest ended **Day 2** → Started **Day 3**

---

## Location 6: The Tower - Marauder Encounter (Day 3, Morning)

### Setup
Upon waking on Day 3, the party noticed 3 tired marauders (variants of [[Marauder Hauler]], [[Marauder Skirmisher|Marauder Skirmisher]], [[Marauder Hunter]]) approaching the tower.

**Positions:**
- Carmilla and Clara: In stealth, positioned outside/near the tower
- Leon: Inside/on the tower, observing

### Encounter Sequence
**Initial Interaction:**
- Carmilla engaged the marauders in conversation
- Marauders claimed they were looking for a place to stay
- Leon observed from the tower and noticed one marauder had their stance ready to draw (wary/prepared)

**Conflict Trigger:**

- Leon shouted a warning to Carmilla and Clara that the marauders were going to attac
- Carmilla created a rift
- Combat began

### Combat Outcome
- Party had numbers advantage
- Marauders were tired (fatigue penalties)
- Party defeated the marauders

### Loot Acquired
- **Fine Full Plate Armour**
- **Fine Recurve Bow**
- **Fine Longsword** (the marauders' equipment were disrupted/acquired)
- **Tarantula parts** (120m of silk, hide, legs, etc.) — the marauders had been harvesting the tarantula

**Post-Combat:**
- Party rested again, recovering spent resources

**Time Update:** Approximately **3/4 of Day 3** passed

---

## Location 7: The Cliff Structure (Day 3, Late)

### Exploration
Party returned to the main chamber and went through to the rift side.

**Environment:**
- Large vertical multi-cliff structure
- Large water pool at the bottom
- Walls covered in webs

**Discovery:**
- A person resting on one of the cliff structures (different cliff from the party's position)
- Person appeared to be a mage

### Approach Attempt
- Leon attempted to wake the person by shooting arrows near them
- First arrow missed
- Second arrow accidentally hit the person in the collarbone

**Crossing:**
- Party used tarantula silk, rope, and other materials
- Created a secured tightrope to reach the person

### Ivan the Mage (Deceased)

**Discovery Details:**
- The mage was dead
- Death was NOT caused by Leon's arrow
- Body appeared drained of all life — unnatural depletion
- Arrow wound was superficial compared to the cause of death

**Items Found:**
- Spellbook (taken by Clara)
- Other loot (taken by Clara)
- Name identified: **Ivan**

**Spellbook Condition:**
- Contents could not be deciphered by the party
- Information made no sense to them
- Clara kept the book

**Additional Detail:**
- A massive magic circle made of ink was present on the ground


### Revealed Information
**Ivan's Context:**
- Ivan was part of the other marauder group
- He and his group used mana stones to power a ritual
- Ivan sacrificed himself (destroyed his soul) to create a massive flood
- The flood killed the tarantula

**Narrative Significance:**
- If the party had gone right instead of left at the burrow, they would have faced the tarantula
- The tarantula was already dead by the time they explored the burrow
- The marauders the party fought had been harvesting from the already-dead tarantula

**Party Action:** Left the cliff structure area with all acquired loot

---

### Return to Main Hall

**Time Update:** Start of **Day 4**
