**Origin Institution:** Runeweavers
**Item Type:** Magic Tool — monocle, worn
**Trigger Mechanism:** Passive — active continuously while worn

## Description
A single brass-rimmed lens on a folding arm, its glass etched with circuitry too fine to see without magnification of its own. Runeweaver commercial catalogues list the Ledgered Eye as a "practical appraisal instrument" — dry, institutional phrasing for what is, in practice, one of their more quietly desirable products among merchants, black-market brokers, and anyone who'd rather not get swindled buying Hollowdeep salvage.

## Mechanical Effect
Unlike most Magic Tools, the Ledgered Eye's second circle doesn't produce a discrete triggered effect — it's a continuous low-draw structure that reads the excitation signature of whatever the wearer looks directly at through the lens for a few seconds. Used on a [[Mana]] Stone, it displays the stone's type (Vivant, Mordant, or Inert) and a rough intensity band without needing an assay point. Used on a suspected magical item, it reveals whether the item is genuinely enchanted or inert, though not the specific effect.

This makes it a favourite of independent buyers who don't want to rely on Ministry-controlled assay pricing, and of anyone burned once too often by a salted fake.

## Limitations
- Cannot identify the specific spell, Enhancement investment, or Defect state of an enchanted item — only whether it's real.
- Reads Mana Stone type and rough intensity, not exact excitation value — a verified assay point is still needed for precise Gilded Mint conversion.
- Requires several seconds of sustained direct focus; not useful for a quick glance in a hurry or in combat.

## Worth
**700 Gilded Mints.** Legal, unremarkable to own, and popular enough in the Market District's grey trade that the Ministry of Balance has quietly discussed regulating its sale — so far without acting on it.
