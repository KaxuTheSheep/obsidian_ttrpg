**Origin Institution:** Elemental Conflux
**Item Type:** Magic Tool — short rod, carried or holstered
**Trigger Mechanism:** A pressure-plate thumb switch along the shaft

## Description
A blackened iron rod roughly the length of a forearm, its tip perpetually faintly discoloured from heat it hasn't produced yet. Conflux apprentices call these "first sparks" — cheap, mass-produced, and usually the first Magic Tool a non-mage expedition member is issued before heading into a nexus with combustible hazards or a need for reliable fire.

## Mechanical Effect
The first circle draws ambient mana; the second accelerates the oxidation rate and thermal energy of fuel at the rod's tip, exactly per the Fire principle in the Conflux's discipline — it does not conjure flame, it needs something to ignite. Pressed against tinder, oil, or a torch head, the trigger reliably produces open flame in a single Tick, no Survival Test required.

Used offensively against a creature or flammable surface within melee reach, it deals **1d6 fire damage** directly, representing a short jet of accelerated combustion rather than a thrown effect.

## Limitations
- Requires fuel to work — useless against bare stone, water, or anything with nothing to burn.
- Recharges fully after 10 Ticks of ambient exposure between uses; can be used again immediately at a Disadvantage on the fire damage roll if triggered before that.
- No range — contact or near-contact only.

## Worth
**220 Gilded Mints.** Deliberately cheap and common — the Conflux produces these in bulk as an entry-level commercial product, and battered secondhand ones circulate through the Market District constantly.
