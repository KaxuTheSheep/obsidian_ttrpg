**Origin Institution:** Lifebinders
**Item Type:** Magic Tool — locket, worn on a chain
**Trigger Mechanism:** Snapping the locket open

## Description
A plain brass locket, warm rather than cold to the touch even in the depths of the Frostvein. Inside, instead of a portrait, sits a small disc of preserved tissue suspended in resin — a donor sample taken specifically to key the circle's biological signature. Lifebinders issue these to expedition medics and to Ashen Oath knights heading into deep nexuses where a Lifebinder can't personally accompany them.

## Mechanical Effect
The first circle gathers ambient mana; the second is keyed to accelerate cellular repair, following the same underlying principle as [[Mending Touch]]-style Lifebinder work, but pre-loaded rather than cast live. Snapping the locket open and pressing it to a wound triggers an immediate **2d6 Blood restoration**, closing the wound as it heals.

Unlike a spell, this does not require a caster present — anyone can trigger it on themselves or an ally, conducting ability or not.

## Limitations
- Single use. Once triggered, the locket's second circle is spent and inert — it cannot be recharged, only remade from scratch by a Lifebinder or Runeweaver with fresh materials.
- Does not remove [[Injuries|wounds]] or treat permanent injury; it restores Blood only.
- Cannot heal a target already at 0 Blood or below — the circle's structure requires the recipient's own cellular processes to still be active to accelerate.

## Worth
**600 Gilded Mints** per locket. Priced deliberately low relative to its effect by the Lifebinders themselves — a policy decision, not a market one, reflecting the Institution's stated priority of keeping expedition medicine accessible rather than exclusive.
