**Origin Institution:** Prismshapers
**Item Type:** Magic Tool — worn ring
**Trigger Mechanism:** Turn the Gear on the side of the ring.

## Description
A small silver ring with ornate elements and a medium sized black spinel. On the side of the ring is a small gear-like fixture which can be used to turn on the spell.

## Mechanical Effect
The first circle gathers ambient mana; Light around the user malforms around the user making them "invisible" the deformation of the light create a haze effect, the ring has 5 levels of invisibility, were every 20% of the gear being turned is 1 level. For every level, the user +2 stealth but for you the light level decreases by 1, this is due to light no longer hitting your eyes. 

## Limitations
- User blinds themself every level.
