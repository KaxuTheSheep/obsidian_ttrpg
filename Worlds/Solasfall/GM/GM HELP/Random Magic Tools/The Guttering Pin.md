**Origin Institution:** Prismshapers
**Item Type:** Magic Tool — cloak pin
**Trigger Mechanism:** A half-turn twist of the pin's head

## Description
A slim silver pin shaped like a guttering candle flame, meant to be worn at the collar or cloak clasp. Prismshaper commercial output rarely reaches the open market — most of their work stays covert or institutional — which makes the handful of Guttering Pins that circulate genuinely sought after among people who need to disappear on short notice and can't wait for a spell.

## Mechanical Effect
The first circle draws ambient mana; the second suppresses light amplitude in a tight zone around the wearer. On activation, light within a **2m radius centred on the wearer** is genuinely suppressed — not obscured, not dimmed, physically absent — for **3 Rounds**. This is real darkness per Prismshaper principles, not an illusion; it cannot be seen through by any means that relies on light reaching the eye.

Anyone standing in the radius when it triggers, wearer included, is plunged into a Black Zone (see [[Vision]]) for the duration.

## Limitations
- Affects everyone in the radius indiscriminately — allies included. It is a tool for a fast, total exit, not a precision effect.
- Single use per Long Rest; the second circle's structure needs a full rebuild by a Runeweaver or equivalent to recharge faster than that.
- The wearer is just as blind as everyone else inside the zone unless they have another means of sensing.

## Worth
**900 Gilded Mints**, though genuine ones rarely sell at listed price — demand among the Thieves' Guild, Rebel cells, and anyone doing work they'd rather the Veiled Watch not witness routinely pushes the real price well past a thousand.
