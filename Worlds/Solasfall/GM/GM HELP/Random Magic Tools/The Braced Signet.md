**Origin Institution:** Aetherbron
**Item Type:** Magic Tool — worn ring
**Trigger Mechanism:** Pressing the signet's face against a flat surface or incoming force

## Description
A heavy iron signet ring, its face stamped with a blank shield rather than any house crest — Aetherbron apprentices are issued these as a graduation piece before they're trusted with anything more dangerous, and most never bother having a crest added. The metal is faintly warm to the touch, humming with a low, contained pressure that never quite settles.

## Mechanical Effect
The ring's first circle draws ambient mana continuously; the second holds a compressed force structure — a barrier, not a punch. When the wearer presses the ring's face flat against a surface (a door, a wall, an incoming blow braced with the forearm) and the trigger engages, a **1m² barrier construct (5 HP)** springs into being at that point, absorbing damage before it reaches the wearer, per the barrier principles in Aetherbron's discipline.

The barrier lasts until its 5 HP is depleted or 5 Ticks pass without it taking damage, at which point it dissipates — it does not recast automatically. The ring requires roughly 10 Ticks of ambient recharge before it can trigger again.

## Limitations
- Only projects the barrier at the point of contact — it cannot be aimed at range.
- The wearer must have a free hand and a clear moment to press the ring against something; it cannot trigger reflexively mid-swing.
- Once depleted, the ring is inert dead weight until it recharges.

## Worth
**450 Gilded Mints.** Aetherbron apprentice work rather than Archmagus-grade — reliable, unglamorous, and common enough among graduated Aetherbron initiates that a few inevitably end up sold or pawned when their owners die or leave the Institution's service.
