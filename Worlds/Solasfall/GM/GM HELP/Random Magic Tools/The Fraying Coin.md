**Origin Institution:** Riftconjurors
**Item Type:** Magic Tool — coin-sized disc, carried
**Trigger Mechanism:** Snapping the coin between thumb and forefinger

## Description
A dull grey disc, slightly warped, etched with a spiral that seems to recede no matter which way it's tilted toward the light. Riftconjurors produce very few of these commercially — most output goes straight to expedition-support contracts with the Explorers' Guild rather than the open market — which makes an independently owned Fraying Coin a genuine rarity outside Guild channels.

## Mechanical Effect
The first circle gathers a dense, localised charge of ambient mana; the second is a pre-stressed spatial structure exploiting the already-open dimensional geometry within the [[Hollowdeep]]'s web, per the Riftconjurors' intra-Hollowdeep rift principle — this only functions inside the Hollowdeep, never in Solasfall proper, since it depends on the web's already-thinned boundaries to work at a survivable mana cost.

Snapping the coin creates a **short-range rift, 8m in any chosen direction**, that the user and up to two adjacent allies may step through instantly. The rift is unstable and collapses the instant the last person passes through it or after 1 Round, whichever comes first.

## Limitations
- Non-functional outside the Hollowdeep's dimensional web.
- Cannot be aimed through solid, undisturbed rock or structure more than roughly a meter thick — it exploits existing weak geometry, it doesn't cut new holes in the world.
- Single use, non-rechargeable. Once snapped, it's spent permanently.

## Worth
**1,400 Gilded Mints.** Rarely sold outright — most that exist are issued directly by the Explorers' Guild to Delver-tier or higher expedition parties as emergency extraction insurance, and a coin found or bought independently is treated as a genuinely valuable find.
