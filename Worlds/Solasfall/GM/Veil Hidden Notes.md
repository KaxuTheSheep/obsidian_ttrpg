The Veil is two concealed systems: a time-dampening barrier and a controlled sky layer. Its true nature is infrastructure, not miracle.

## The Time Threat
The outside world is in equilibrium contact with a fast-time realm. Those beyond the Veil do not perceive their acceleration—their minds experience normal time while their bodies age and deteriorate in moments. The Veil suppresses this equilibrium pull via a sustained Mordant-excited mana field; without it, the city synchronises with external decay.

## Construction & Cost**
The Veil was sealed by the current King. Conducting the required mana through a single soul caused catastrophic Mana Overload Syndrome, permanently severing his connection to mana. The network is infrastructure—operators monitor flow; they do not power it directly. The King's very public injury seeded the soul-rumour.

## The Barrier's Physics
The boundary is a pressure gradient, not a wall. Interior mana is partially isolated from the World's Breath and neutralises toward inert without replenishment. Outside, the World's Breath pushes mana to extremes. The sharp differential creates resistance. At the fringes, the sky layer thins and density becomes perceptible—heaviness, wrongness in light, mist-like boundary, glimpses of the Fallows.

## Sky Layer as Control
The Vivant mana field manipulates light wavelengths directly. Royal Advisors use weather conditions as an implicit political language—associating light with decrees or ceremonies to simulate divine favour. Wealthy nobles can petition and pay for bespoke skies. The sky layer also extends beyond the outer walls, covertly sustaining farms and rivers that would otherwise face acceleration.

## Staffing Pressure
The rotation pool is ~80–100 mages. Losing qualified operators to Hollowdeep expeditions or injury creates measurable strain on shift coverage—a hidden pressure on Ministry resource allocation.

## The Rift Cost
The Veil and the Rift opening compete for the same mana environment. The four-week weakening is not an accident—it is an accepted, managed cost to free mana for the Riftconjurors. Renewal ceremonies are moments of visible fragility, when rumours of failure circulate most freely.

## Economic Burden
Mordant Stone taxation falls heaviest on poorer districts, who benefit least from the sky layer's political uses. Growing dissent exists. The Rebel Group is divided: some believe freedom lies beyond the Veil (ignoring or disbelieving the acceleration); others know the true threat and fear any weakening courts catastrophe.

**Deliberate Manipulation**: The soul-sustenance rumour is false, but the Royal Mages have never denied it. A populace that believes the Veil demands souls is less likely to question its maintenance fees. The sky layer's management of familiar rhythms is calculated—not accidental—to manufacture acceptance.
