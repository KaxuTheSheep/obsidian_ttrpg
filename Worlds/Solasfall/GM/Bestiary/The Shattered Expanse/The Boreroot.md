*Petrified, until it isn't.*

Long, hard-segmented, and utterly still, the Boreroot is indistinguishable from the forest of stone trunks it anchors itself among — until something lingers close enough for its bore head to find purchase. It does not chase. It does not need to. Whatever it strikes rarely walks away without knowing exactly how much force a "tree" can carry.

Found rooted throughout [[The Shattered Expanse]]'s petrified treeline, and occasionally near membrane seams, where its bore head has been known to punch clean through the membrane itself when sufficiently provoked.

## [[Movement Types|Movement]]
The Boreroot is rooted and has no Movement values — it cannot voluntarily relocate. Its segmented body allows it to lean, extend, and strike within a fixed radius of its anchor point without this counting as movement.

## Petrified Camouflage (Passive)
Indistinguishable from the surrounding stone trunks while dormant. A creature searching the area must succeed a **[[Skills|Perception]] Test (DN 15)** to identify the Boreroot before it strikes; searching specifically for ambush threats (rather than general awareness) grants **Advantage** on this Test. The Boreroot does not attack while unnoticed unless a creature comes within its strike radius — see **Reach**.

## [[Actions]]

### Bore Strike
*The drill head snaps forward on a segmented spine built for exactly one motion.*
**[[Damage Types|Damage]]:** 2d8 + [[Attributes|Might]] [[Damage Types|Piercing]]
**[[Ticks, Priority & Resolution|Priority]]:** 8 + [[Attributes|Might]]
**[[Range & Threat|Range]]:** +2m (Reach)
**Type:** Melee
**Traits:** Sundering — a successful Bore Strike against an armoured target immediately drops that armour 1 Quality Tier, per [[Weapon Traits#Sundering|Sundering]].

### Membrane Punch (Situational)
*Provoked or cornered, the drill head finds the nearest membrane instead of the nearest flesh.*
Once per encounter, if a membrane is within the Boreroot's Reach, it may drive its bore head through it instead of attacking a creature. This does not damage anything on the far side automatically, but leaves a temporary rupture the GM may rule affects that membrane's tension or Reflection Linkage for the remainder of the scene — see [[The Shattered Expanse]] for membrane mechanics.

## Behaviour
The Boreroot does not pursue. It strikes anything within its fixed Reach radius and otherwise remains dormant, making it a positional hazard rather than a combatant in the conventional sense — parties who identify and route around it face no threat at all. It does not distinguish between provocation types; damage, proximity, or disturbing its root bed are all sufficient triggers. If reduced below 25% Blood, it will attempt Membrane Punch if a membrane is in range, preferring to rupture its surroundings over dying quietly.

## Nexus Notes
Its camouflage and stillness make the Boreroot a natural "check before you rest" teaching tool for parties new to the Expanse. A Boreroot rooted near a membrane seam is also a convenient diegetic explanation if the GM wants a particular face's rift connection to read as damaged or unstable without needing a larger plot reason.
