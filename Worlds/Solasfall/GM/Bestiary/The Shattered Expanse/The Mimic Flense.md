*Nothing native to the Sunken Hollow or above. Something the membranes taught to lie.*

A loose, greyish mass beneath its assumed shape, the Flense has no true form of its own — only the shapes it has taken from things it has watched die near a membrane. It does not hunt by chasing. It hunts by waiting until something is alone, wounded, or separated, then wearing that thing's face back into the party that lost them.

Found near membrane seams throughout [[The Shattered Expanse]], most often close to the Central Cube where stragglers are likeliest to be briefly alone.

## Mimicry (Passive)
While disguised, the Flense perfectly copies the appearance, wounds, missing equipment, gait, and voice of a specific creature it has observed — typically a downed, wounded, or separated party member. This is not illusion; its body has physically reshaped to match. A creature interacting with it must succeed an **[[Attributes|Insight]] Test (DN 15)** to notice anything is wrong, and even then only if given specific cause for suspicion (the real target turning up alive, contradictory behaviour, and so on). Passive observation alone does not grant a Test — the GM should call for one only when the fiction gives a character reason to look closely.

While mimicking, the Flense does not initiate combat and will actively avoid situations that risk exposing it early, including retreating from a fight it could otherwise win.

*The instant it commits, its assumed shape tears open along seams that were never really skin.*
As a **Full [[Actions|Action]]**, the Flense abandons its disguise and immediately makes a **Split Maw** [[Attacks|attack]] against the creature it was closest to or interacting with, gaining **Advantage** if that creature had not detected the ruse.

## True Form
Once Split Maw triggers, the disguise cannot be reformed for the remainder of the encounter. In its true form the Flense is slow and comparatively fragile — halve its Walk and Run Travel Rates, and it loses access to Mimicry entirely. It fights only because it is cornered at this point, not because open combat favours it; a Flense given a viable escape route will usually take it rather than press an unwinnable fight.

## Behaviour
The Flense is patient to the point of passivity — it will maintain a disguise for hours if undisturbed, waiting for an opening rather than forcing one. It does not attack indiscriminately; it targets whoever is most isolated from the group, or whoever would cause the most disruption if suddenly revealed as a threat (a healer, a lookout, someone holding a choke point). Once exposed, it commits fully to Split Maw and then fights defensively, prioritising escape back through a membrane over a fight it cannot win in its true form.

## Nexus Notes
See [[The Shattered Expanse]] for the full membrane mechanics this creature exploits. A Flense that escapes through a membrane mid-encounter should be treated as gone for that scene — chasing it into an unstable membrane crossing is rarely worth the risk it implies.
