*Not hostile. Not friendly. Hungry, in a way that doesn't map cleanly onto either.*

A floating orb of membrane-substance, ferrofluid-slick and constantly shifting, the Droplet is closer to a living extension of the membranes themselves than a conventional creature. It can adhere to or peel away from any surface — floor, wall, another membrane, a person — the way a water droplet chooses where to sit rather than where it's told. Left alone, it drifts, feeding quietly on whatever ambient strangeness the Expanse provides.

Found drifting near membrane seams throughout [[The Shattered Expanse]], with no fixed territory.

## Free-Form Body (Passive)
The Droplet has no fixed shape and can reshape part of itself into a spike, limb, or blunted mass as a **free action** once per [[Rounds|Round]]. It can freely change its size from Tiny up to Small creature ignoring any Health Changes. 

## Sustenance (Passive)
The Droplet consumes mana, this mana can come in many forms since most stuff contains a small amount of it, the ambient mana of the Expanse is the minimal amount of sustenance for it to go in a hibernetic form. To stay in its normal form.

## Hibernation (Passive)
The Droplet can go in a hibernetic state where it adheres to a surface and stays still consuming ambient mana keeping itself alive.

## Behaviour
Docile by default. A Droplet that has fed recently will drift past creatures without incident, and may even be induced to stabilise a nearby membrane's tension or dampen an impending Reflection Sanity check if "fed" deliberately (a character voluntarily offering Sanity, or a burst of expended mana nearby) — GM's discretion on exact terms, but this should be a genuine option, not a trick. A starved Droplet grows erratic, attempting Draw Charge opportunistically and escalating to Spike Lash only if that fails repeatedly or it's directly attacked first.

It does not hold grudges in any conventional sense and will disengage the moment it's sated, mid-combat if necessary.
