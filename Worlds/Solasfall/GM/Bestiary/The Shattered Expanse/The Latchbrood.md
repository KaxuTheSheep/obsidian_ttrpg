*Small. Numerous. Not quite where you last looked.*

Insectoid and unremarkable individually, the Latchbrood has adapted to live inside the 3-second gap between a membrane and its reflection — phasing between the delayed and live states such that, to a normal observer, they appear to blink in and out of existence rather than simply move quickly. A swarm of them is rarely lethal on its own, but disorienting enough to open the door for something worse.

Found in loose swarms throughout [[The Shattered Expanse]], especially near membrane seams and the Central Cube's ceiling.

## Phase-Step (Passive)
A Latchbrood exists partially out of sync with normal time, flickering between its delayed and live state. This grants it **Advantage on Evasion against the first [[Attacks|attack]] made against it each [[Rounds|Round]]** — by the time a blow lands, it may already be three seconds elsewhere. This bonus does not apply against attacks that specifically target its delayed-state position (GM discretion — an attacker deliberately reading its membrane-reflection to predict its "real" position bypasses this entirely).

## Swarm Behaviour
Latchbrood are encountered in groups of 4–10. Rather than tracking each individually in a large group, the GM may treat a swarm as a single pool of Blood (individual Blood × count) for speed, with Latch Bite attacks scaling in number with remaining swarm size at GM discretion. Individually fragile, they compensate through numbers and Phase-Step's Evasion bonus, which applies per-individual even when tracked as a pool.

## Behaviour
Opportunistic and non-tactical. Latchbrood swarm toward disturbance, noise, or an already-wounded target, but scatter and phase away rather than pressing an unfavourable fight — they do not have the individual toughness to commit the way a Boreroot or Flense might. Their real danger is less the damage they deal and more the confusion: a party mid-fight against something else, suddenly also fending off things that "weren't there a second ago," is exactly the scenario they're built to create.

## Nexus Notes
Low-stakes trash-encounter filler, intended so not every fight in the Expanse is a set-piece. Works well layered underneath a [[The Boreroot|Boreroot]] ambush or as a distraction while a [[The Mimic Flense|Mimic Flense]] closes in unnoticed.
