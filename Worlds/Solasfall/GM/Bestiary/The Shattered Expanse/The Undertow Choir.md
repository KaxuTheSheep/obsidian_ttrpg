*Not a creature. Not quite nothing, either.*

The Undertow Choir isn't a monster and has no stat block — it's a passive phenomenon that occurs near any membrane under elevated tension: post-damage (a [[The Boreroot|Boreroot]] strike gone wide, a failed accidental-passage Test), or simply inherent to a highly active membrane like the elastic West face. Faint, overlapping whispers of a character's own voice bleed through from the delayed reflection, saying things that are almost — but not quite — what was actually said a few seconds ago.

## Trigger
At GM discretion, when a party lingers near a tensioned membrane (post-combat, mid-rest, or simply exploring too close for too long), call for a **Resolve Test (DN 14)** per the standard Reflection Sanity rule in [[The Shattered Expanse]]. No creature, no combat, no warning beyond the whispers themselves starting.

## Use
The Choir exists to pace dread between set-piece encounters without requiring a monster to justify every unsettling moment. It's cheap, repeatable, and entirely GM-timed — use it to punctuate exploration, especially in the Central Cube or near a face membrane the party hasn't crossed yet, rather than saving all tension for combat.

## Nexus Notes
See [[The Shattered Expanse]] for full membrane and Reflection Sanity mechanics. The Choir does not scale with "number of Reflections visible" the way a direct look at a membrane does — treat it as a flat single Resolve Test regardless of how many characters are present, since the whispering is ambient rather than a direct mirror-confrontation.
