The soul is not mystical—it is an emergent, stable internal mana current generated by neurological activity. This current is the sole enabler of active mana control; without it, creatures are only passively affected by mana.

## Origin
The brain's electrochemical processes cohere ambient mana into a persistent current flowing through the nervous system. It is continuously generated, not stored.

## Signature vs Substance
The observable mana signature is the soul's ambient expression, not the soul itself, and reflects biology, experience, and identity. Upon death in high-density mana (e.g., the Hollowdeep), this signature crystallises into an Essence instead of dissipating—a process governed by local density, not neurological complexity.

## Conduction & Propagation
A mage uses the soul as a sorting structure to direct ambient particles; mana generation is impossible. The current extends through the entire nervous system. Consuming an Essence alters this current; the altered pattern propagates outward, compelling the body to physically adapt (tissue density, physiology) to the new signature. Connection persists only while the neural pathway remains intact.

## Death
Neural cessation collapses the current to dispersion. Only exceptionally dense ambient mana can interrupt this, triggering crystallisation.

## Deities (Constructs)
Deities are mana constructs sustained by collective neurological output (belief). A Follower's current synchronises with this construct via a resonant link—divine power flows through this touch, not through the Follower's own conduction. The link is two-way; sustained synchronisation subtly alters the Follower's current over time, manifesting mechanically as class abilities and narratively as shifted disposition.

## Essence Integration
Absorbed fragments merge wholly into the host current. The soul is not selective—drawbacks and benefits are inseparably fused as a single whole pattern. Essences cannot be removed; they are only displaced by attunement to a new one during a Long Rest.
