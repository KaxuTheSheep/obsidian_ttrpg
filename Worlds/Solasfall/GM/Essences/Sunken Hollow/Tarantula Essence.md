**Source Creature:** [[Tarantula]]
**Essence Name:** The Patient Bite

## Core Concept
The Tarantula's signature is built almost entirely around stillness rewarded. Absorbing it does not make you stronger in a fight already underway — it makes the moments before a fight feel different. Recipients report a background hum of ground-vibration awareness they never asked for, and a bite reflex that activates faster than conscious thought when something finally gets close enough.

## Benefits
- **+1 Might**, reflecting the raw crushing force behind the bite.
- Gain a passive, weaker version of **Vibration Sense**: against a creature moving at Walk pace or slower who has made no significant noise, your Perception Tests targeting them are made with **Advantage** instead of the Tarantula's full detection suite — you sense the tremor, not the precise position.
- Once per Rest, you may make a **Fanged Bite** unarmed attack: 1d8 + Might Piercing, Priority 8 + Might. On hit, the target makes a Vitality Test (DN 12) or gains Disadvantage on Might and Grace Tests for 1d4 Rounds.

## Drawback
Your eyesight dulls to match the source — you gain **Disadvantage on all sight-based Perception Tests in Light Level 3 or above**, the signature's total disinterest in vision at range bleeding into your own senses. You also find sustained combat uncomfortable in a way you can't quite name; if a single fight runs past 5 Rounds without you disengaging or resting, you take **Disadvantage on your next Test** at the start of each subsequent Round, an inherited territorial instinct to retreat and reset rather than commit.

## GM Notes
Strong essence — the Tarantula is the apex of its ecosystem and the ability set (ambush detection, a genuine bite attack, hard Attribute bonus) reflects that. Should be a meaningfully sought-after drop, not a common one; consider requiring the party to have actually fought and killed the Tarantula itself (not scavenged it dead, as happened in Session 1) for it to manifest at all — a corpse already drained by Ivan's flood may not carry enough signature density to crystallise.
