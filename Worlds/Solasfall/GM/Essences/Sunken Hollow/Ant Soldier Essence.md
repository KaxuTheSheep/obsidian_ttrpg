**Source Creature:** [[Ant Soldier]]
**Essence Name:** The Colony's Certainty

## Core Concept
The Ant Soldier's signature carries almost none of its own individuality — it is a fragment of a much larger collective pattern, blunt and utterly without hesitation. Recipients don't gain cunning or subtlety from it. They gain the total absence of doubt a soldier caste feels when committing to a fight it was bred for.

## Benefits
- **+1 Vitality.**
- Your bite (or any unarmed strike, reflavoured as a bite) may apply a mild acid/formic burn on hit: target makes a Vitality Test (DN 11) or takes **Burn** for 1 Round (1d2 fire damage, bypasses Sturdiness).
- You gain **Advantage on Tests to resist being knocked Prone, forcibly moved, or Frightened** while an ally is within your melee threat range — the colony does not break formation alone.

## Drawback
You inherit the caste's complete lack of self-preservation instinct. You **cannot voluntarily Withdraw or disengage from melee** once you have committed to an Attack against a target this encounter, unless an ally explicitly orders you to or you are reduced below 20% Blood. This is not a roleplay suggestion — treat it as a hard mechanical restriction, same weight as a Condition.

## GM Notes
Middling power, deliberately blunt — reflects a soldier-caste creature that is dangerous in numbers and unremarkable alone. The Drawback is the interesting part of this one and should be enforced strictly; a player who wants a tankier melee frontline build gets real value, but the essence actively fights anyone who wants tactical flexibility.
