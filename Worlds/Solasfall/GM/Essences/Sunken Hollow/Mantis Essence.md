**Source Creature:** [[Mantis]]
**Essence Name:** The Waiting Colour

## Core Concept
Where the Tarantula's signature is about ambush through stillness, the Mantis's is about ambush through disguise — a body that doesn't just hide but actively lies about what it is until the moment it chooses not to. Recipients describe a faint, involuntary skin-crawl in low light, as if their own colouring wants to shift and can't quite manage it without training.

## Benefits
- **+1 Grace.**
- Gain **+2 to Stealth Tests** while in Light Level 2 or below and stationary — a diminished, trainable echo of the Mantis's full Adaptive Camouflage rather than the automatic version.
- The first Attack Test you make against a target that has not detected you each encounter gains **Advantage**, even if you would already have Advantage from Stealth rules — this stacks narratively as "doubly certain" rather than mechanically doubling.

## Drawback
Your body's colour genuinely destabilises under strong emotion or stress the same way the Mantis's does — when you gain **Frightened**, or drop below 30% Blood, your skin visibly shifts through unnatural colour for 1 Round, which **ends any active Stealth or Disguise you're maintaining automatically**, regardless of whether anyone was looking. You have no control over this.

## GM Notes
A mid-strength essence built for synergy rather than raw power — genuinely excellent for a Stealth or Assassin-leaning build, close to useless for anyone else, which is exactly the design intent from the "synergy over power" brief. Don't be tempted to buff the numbers to make it more broadly appealing; the whole point is that it rewards a specific playstyle rather than being universally good.
