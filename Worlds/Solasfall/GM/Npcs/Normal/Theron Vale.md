**Role:** Independent alchemist operating out of a repurposed temple-foyer in Solasfall's lower district. Publically: sells potions, poultices, and "safe" alchemical reagents. Privately: experiments with Hollowdeep-tainted materials, seeking to distill their properties without the corruption.  
**Status:** Alive (for now)  
**Race:** Human (middle-aged, weathered, with the kind of tired eyes)  
**Languages:** Common, Solari

## Name
Theron Vale was not born with that name. Records from the Guild of Alchemists show a "Theron Vail" — one 'i' — who was quietly expelled two decades ago for "unsafe experimentation" and "failure to observe containment protocols." The name change was his own, a small vanity meant to suggest refinement and old money that his family never had. He insists the "Vale" comes from a noble line in the old Solari territories; he cannot produce a single document to support this. When pressed, he changes the subject by offering a free sample of his latest tincture.

## Overview
Theron Vale operates from a converted temple-antechamber in Solasfall's lower market district — the kind of neighbourhood where rents are cheap and neighbours have learned not to ask about the smells. The space still bears faded ceiling frescos of a sun-god nobody worships anymore, now mostly hidden behind shelves of mismatched glassware, drying herbs, and cages of small creatures that are definitely not all still alive.

He is an alchemist in the truest sense: endlessly curious, deeply pragmatic about ethical boundaries, and utterly convinced that he is one breakthrough away from vindication. His public-facing business keeps the lights on: basic healing potions, antitoxins, fire salves — the standard adventurer's kit, priced fairly enough that he gets repeat customers from the Guild. His private work, conducted in a back room he refers to as "the study" and everybody else calls "the reason we don't open that door," is where his true passion lies.

That passion is alchemical transmutation of Hollowdeep materials. Theron believes — with the fervour of a convert — that the Hollowdeep's corruption is not inherently evil, merely _unstable_, and that with the right catalysts and methodology, it could be refined into something constructive. He wants to _tame_ the Hollowdeep's residue, not merely survive it. This conviction cost him his Guild standing twenty years ago and has cost him most of his reputation since.

He advertises for apprentices constantly, in the way a man drowning advertises for someone to throw him a rope. The posting on the Guild board is perennial, its wording shifting every few months between desperate ("Learn from a true master!") and defensive ("I don't need help, I'm looking to mentor") depending on how his latest experiment went. The apprenticeship is real in theory; in practice, his last three assistants left under various clouds, and the one before that was last seen heading toward the Hollowdeep with a vial of something he was specifically told not to test on living tissue.

## Personality
- **Obsessive.** Theron's mind works like a spiral — he can hold a normal conversation for about two minutes before drifting back to his research, and once he starts explaining his theories he will _not_ stop unless physically interrupted. He has no social off-switch for his special interests.
- **Earnest to a fault.** He is not deceptive about his work; he will tell anyone who asks exactly what he's doing, consequences be damned, because he believes the work itself is righteous and the dangers are merely _challenges to be overcome_. This makes him simultaneously more honest and more dangerous than most merchants.
- **Defensive about his failures.** Despite his openness about his theories, he becomes clipped and cold when asked about his expelled assistants or his Guild expulsion. He has a script for this — "professional differences," "they lacked vision," "the Guild is conservative" — and recites it in a tone that dares argument.
- **Genuinely talented, genuinely reckless.** Theron is not a fraud. His basic potions are excellent, his theoretical knowledge is deep, and he can identify Hollowdeep-tainted materials by smell alone. The problem is that his talent is married to a conviction that he can control forces that have killed better alchemists through caution alone.
- **Touchingly earnest about mentorship.** He _wants_ to teach. He genuinely believes he has knowledge worth passing on. The fact that he's a terrible teacher — impatient, over-explanatory, prone to assuming his apprentice knows the basics — doesn't diminish the sincerity of the impulse. He lights up when someone expresses interest.

## The Study (His Workshop)
Theron's workshop is a converted back room approximately twelve feet square, accessible only through a reinforced door he keeps locked. The key is on his person at all times, which is a deliberate choice: he used to leave it in a drawer, until an incident where the drawer was opened by a curious apprentice.

Inside:
- Three workbenches, each cluttered with glassware, mortars, and drying racks.
- A locked cabinet containing his Hollowdeep samples, each in a labelled containment vessel. The labels are in his own notation system; a successful DC 15 Investigation check (or alchemical knowledge) reveals they're organised by origin location within the Hollowdeep.
- A large mirror on one wall, cracked and tarnished, with a small shelf beneath it holding a comb and a tin of moustache wax. He is a little vain about his appearance, despite his general dishevelment.
- A cot in the corner, rarely used — he often sleeps at his workbench.
- A single framed sketch on the wall: a woman's face, faded, worn at the corners. He does not discuss it.
The room is ordered chaos. Theron knows exactly where everything is and becomes agitated if moved.

## Apprentices
Theron has had five apprentices in twenty years. Three left voluntarily, citing "philosophical differences" (meaning: they found his work too dangerous). One was expelled after attempting to sell his notes to the Guild. One disappeared in the Hollowdeep, having taken a sample from the locked cabinet against explicit instruction.

He is currently seeking a sixth. His advertisement reads:

> **APPRENTICE WANTED** — ALCHEMICAL STUDIES, PRACTICAL & THEORETICAL. MUST BE COMFORTABLE WITH NON-STANDARD REAGENTS AND UNUSUAL METHODOLOGIES. INQUIRE AT THE TEMPLE-FOYER, LOWER MARKET DISTRICT. NO GUILD AFFILIATION REQUIRED. NO COWARDICE.

The last line is a recent addition, added after the last apprentice left.
