Bobby "Bob" West, a person forged through trials and challenges. He was raised by his parents, who inherently were on the poorer side of society. The previous generations of his family were rich, but their money was lost due to unknown reasons. Despite their financial troubles, they did their best to ensure Bobby could live a good life, he grew up in a school, with his parents expecting to see him go to college, however, one day tragedy struck as Bobby's parents were put on trial as they were suspected to be a part of the Rebel's, a group notoriously known for being against [[Worlds/Solasfall/GM/Factions/The Ministry of Balance|the ministry]] of balance. After their trial, they were indiscriminately executed, their old age meant they weren't as able bodied to go into [[hollowdeep]], so Bobby had to fend for himself at the age of 18, doing what he could to get by, and he barely managed to, but after 2 years, while he was walking around the city, he heard crying, he went into an alleyway and saw two children, knowing he would regret if he left them, he made the tough decision to take them under his care, and for many years he barely managed to, but as he turned 40, he found it harder to find work, only being able to do one job which didn't pay him enough to cover their expenses, he chose to avoid paying his taxes, after 3 years he was arrested by the [[Order of the Gilded Chain]] after they noticed a discrepancy in his documents which unveiled he had avoided his taxes for years, he was put on trial and he expected to meet the same fate as his parents, but he had an old friend from when he was working back in his 20's, although he cut ties with said friend when he looked for a new job, he did do a favour for his friend, and to repay it, his friend influenced the trial and gave him the option to pay off his debt by going into [[Hollowdeep]] and mining [[mana]] crystals. He accepted this deal, and he now has a large debt to pay off, which would certainly take at least a year, maybe multiple to pay off what he owes.
