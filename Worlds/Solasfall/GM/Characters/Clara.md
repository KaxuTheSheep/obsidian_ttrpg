Clara was raised by her two parents in a wealthy and well respected family, until a little after her sister ([[Carmilla]]) was born... her father found out that her mother had an affair and due to the risk of losing their respect, he swept his wife and her "bastard" children under the rug, as if they never existed.

Shortly after being abandoned, Clara and her sister was left alone by their mother too, her location and status unknown, and without anyone to look after her sister, Clara decided she would protect her...

After a few difficult days, a thoughtful human, [[Bob]], took the two sisters in, treating them as his own daughters, even though he was financially burdened, he was a better father than the sister's previous one. He gave them a fruitful life and made sure they both happy, even 20 years later.

Unfortunately, one day Clara's sister became a Velkhari after a tragic incident, making her forget all the amazing memories she made with Clara and her father, and ultimately caused her wedding to be crashed by her infected sister... 

Clara couldn't cope with losing her sibling, and so when she found out her father was sentenced to go into [[Hollowdeep]], she decided to tag along with him in hopes to find a way to revert her sister back to human.
